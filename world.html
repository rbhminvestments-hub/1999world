Why 1999?

1999 isn’t about going backwards.

It’s about remembering when the internet felt like somewhere you explored, rather than somewhere that followed you everywhere.

Why we’re here

The world became connected.
We became disconnected.

The world became faster.
We forgot how to wait.

The world became louder.
We stopped listening.

The internet we miss

Small websites. Strange corners. Personal pages. Long emails.

Places made by people, not shaped by algorithms.

The future we’re building

Digital is the doorway. Real life is the destination.

We’re bringing forward the parts of life worth keeping: curiosity, creativity, conversation and Sundays with nowhere else to be.
